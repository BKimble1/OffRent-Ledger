Privacy Policy

OffRent Ledger · Idlery Services LLC · Effective date: on first App Store release. Version 1.0.

The short version

OffRent Ledger keeps everything you enter on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no analytics. We do not collect your rentals, your documents, your photos or your location, because there is nowhere for them to go.

Three features draw on Apple's own services and nothing else does: the maps, searching for a jobsite address, and buying a subscription. What that involves is set out under What goes to Apple below.

What we collect

Nothing. Idlery Services LLC receives no data from this app.

The app has no login, no user account, no backend service, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no attribution SDK, no third-party crash reporter and no tracking of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

If you email our support address, we receive whatever you choose to put in that email. That is a normal email, handled outside the app.

What stays on your device

  • Rentals, vendors, jobsites, agreements, timeline events, invoices and follow-ups, in a database in the app's own storage area.
  • Photographs and documents you scan, choose or import, in the app's own file area, protected by iOS file protection.
  • Reminder settings and your subscription status, in the app's preferences.

Anything the app writes is inside its own container, which iOS keeps separate from other apps. Deleting OffRent Ledger removes all of it.

Permissions, and why

Camera — requested the first time you tap a scan or photo button. Used to scan rental contracts and invoices and to photograph equipment condition, meters and fuel gauges. Scanning and text recognition happen entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No image and no recognised text is ever sent anywhere.

Photos — the app uses the system photo picker, which lets you choose specific images without granting access to your library. If you ask the app to save a file back to Photos, iOS asks for add-only permission at that moment.

Location — requested only when you tap "Add current location" while recording an off-rent confirmation or a pickup. It takes one reading, in the foreground, at approximately 100-metre accuracy, and stores it with that single record. The app does not monitor your location in the background, does not keep any route or location history, and does not request always-on access. Declining does not block anything.

Notifications — requested only after you turn on a reminder in Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. The app receives no push notifications and has no server that could send one.

What goes to Apple

The app has no server of its own, but three of its features are built on Apple frameworks that talk to Apple. Idlery Services LLC never sees any of it; Apple's Privacy Policy governs it.

  • Maps. Whenever a map is on screen — the card on Today, the full-screen rental map, and the jobsite picker — Apple's Maps service supplies the map imagery, the same way it does in Apple's own Maps app.
  • Searching for a jobsite. When you type an address or a place into the jobsite picker, that text is sent to Apple's place search, along with the area the map is currently showing so the results are near what you are looking at. Your device's own location is *not* used for this, and no rental, amount, company or document is ever attached to a search.
  • Naming a dropped pin. When you place a pin by hand, the app asks Apple for the nearest address to that one coordinate, so it can suggest a name rather than leaving you looking at numbers. It happens once, for that pin, and only when you drop one.
  • Subscriptions. Handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit — see Purchases below.

Reading your documents is *not* on this list. Text recognition runs on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework, and where your iPhone supports it the on-device language model runs there too. No page, photograph or line of recognised text is ever uploaded to anyone, including us.

What we do not do

  • No data leaves your device for us. The three Apple services above are the only outbound requests the app can make, and none of them carries a rental, an amount or a document.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or telemetry, first-party or third-party.
  • No crash reporting SDK.
  • No cloud OCR, machine learning service, or AI service of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we hold none.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making about you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. OffRent Ledger asks StoreKit whether you have an active subscription and stores that answer on your device. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or any personal information about you. Apple's own privacy policy governs the purchase itself.

Your data, your control

  • Delete any individual attachment.
  • Delete any rental, which deletes its events, photos and documents.
  • Delete everything, from Settings › Data and privacy, which removes every record and every file from this iPhone.
  • Export a CSV summary, a full JSON backup, and a per-rental PDF at any time. Exporting is not behind a subscription.

Because there is no server, deletion on your device is deletion everywhere. It also means we cannot recover anything for you. Export before you delete the app.

Children

OffRent Ledger is a tool for construction businesses. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anybody.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships inside the app and its effective date changes. The app's privacy label on the App Store is kept consistent with this document.
