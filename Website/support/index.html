Common questions

Getting started

Install OffRent Ledger on an iPhone running iOS 18 or later, open it, and add your first rental. There is no account to create, no email to verify and no password. Nothing is sent anywhere, so there is nothing to sign in to.

The free tier covers one open rental at a time, with the complete workflow available on it.

Adding or scanning a rental

Tap the add button and either type the details in or tap Scan the contract to use the camera. You can also import a PDF or pick a photo you already have.

Text recognition runs entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded. Whatever it reads is shown to you on a review screen with a tick beside each value: only the values you tick are saved, and you can correct any of them first. A scan on its own never writes anything.

The rates and dates you enter are what every later estimate is calculated from, so it is worth checking them against the contract.

Recording an off-rent confirmation

When you have finished with a machine, mark it done. The app moves it to Contact vendor and offers you the rental company's phone number, email or link — you make the contact. OffRent Ledger does not contact rental companies and does not end rentals.

When the yard gives you a confirmation, record it: the confirmation number, who you spoke to, how you reached them, and the time. You can add a one-off location reading if you want one. The rental then shows Vendor confirmation recorded, and the running estimate stops accruing at that moment rather than at pickup.

If they would not give you a confirmation number, you can record that too — the app asks you to acknowledge it rather than leaving the field silently blank.

Tracking pickup

Confirmed off rent is not the same as collected, so the rental stays on the Awaiting pickup list until you record the pickup.

When the machine actually leaves, record the pickup with the hour or mile reading, the fuel level, and photographs of the condition. That is the state of the machine when it left your site, kept with the rental.

Reviewing an invoice discrepancy

Attach the final invoice to the rental — scan it, import a PDF, or enter the lines by hand. The app compares it against the terms you confirmed and reports any possible invoice mismatch: an extra rental day, a rate that is not the one you entered, a line it has no way to derive.

A possible mismatch is a prompt to look, and nothing more. The app does not determine that a charge is incorrect, unauthorised or unlawful, and it is not accounting or legal advice. Whether to accept a charge, question it or dispute it is entirely your decision.

If you decide to question one, export an evidence packet (below) and take it up with the rental company directly.

Exporting evidence

Three exports, none of them behind the subscription:

  • PDF packet — one rental, with its timeline, the confirmation you recorded, the invoice comparison and the photographs you attached.
  • CSV summary — every rental as a row, for a spreadsheet.
  • JSON backup — everything, in a format the app can import again.

The PDF is a summary of information you entered and files you captured. Timestamps reflect when things were entered on your device. They are not independently witnessed and are not legally binding. Read anything you generate before you send it to anyone.

Restoring purchases

Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases. This asks Apple whether the Apple Account signed in on this device has an active subscription, and updates the app accordingly. It works on a new iPhone, after a reinstall, and after a restore from backup.

If nothing is restored, check that the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought the subscription.

Managing or cancelling an Apple subscription

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel yours in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or from the link in the app's Subscription screen.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies — we cannot issue them.

Ending a subscription never removes or hides anything you have already recorded. You keep viewing, editing, resolving, exporting and deleting all of it. Without Pro you cannot open an additional rental beyond the free limit.

Camera, photo and notification permissions

Camera — asked for the first time you tap a scan or photo button. Used to scan contracts and invoices and to photograph condition, meters and fuel gauges.

Photos — the app uses the system picker, which lets you choose specific images without granting access to your whole library. Add-only permission is requested only if you ask the app to save a file back to Photos.

Location — asked only when you tap Add current location while recording a confirmation or a pickup. One reading, in the foreground, stored with that single record. No background tracking and no location history. Declining blocks nothing.

Notifications — asked only after you turn on a reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone; there is no server that could push one.

Deleting your data

Delete an individual attachment, delete a rental (which deletes its events, photographs and documents), or delete everything from Settings › Data and privacy.

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of it as well.

Because there is no server, deletion on your device is deletion everywhere — which also means we cannot recover anything for you. Export before you delete.

Privacy and where your data lives

Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no crash-reporting SDK. Idlery Services LLC receives no data from the app.

The app works with no signal at all, which is the normal condition in a yard or a basement.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

OffRent Ledger does not contact rental companies and does not end rentals. It keeps your record of what was agreed, what you were told and when. The rental company's own agreement governs your rental.
