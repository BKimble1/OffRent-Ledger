The problem

Calling it off rent is not the end of the money.

You phone the yard on a Friday and tell them you are done with the skid steer. Then the machine sits on site until Wednesday. The invoice arrives three weeks later with an extra week on it, and nobody can remember who you spoke to, or when.

The call is not written down

A confirmation number on the back of a receipt is not a record. Two months later, neither is a memory of the call.

Pickup is a separate event

Off rent and collected are different days. Which one the invoice bills to is worth knowing before you pay it.

The invoice arrives cold

Checking a final invoice means reconstructing dates and rates from memory — which is why most of them are simply paid.

What it does

Six things, done properly.

Every figure comes from the rates and dates you entered. The app does not interpret your contract and does not decide anything for you.

See what is running now

Estimated rent running across every open rental, from the delivery date and the rates you entered. One number on the Today screen, updated as the days pass.

See the next rate change coming

Daily to weekly, weekly to four-week — the app shows when the next roll-over falls and what it is expected to add, so a decision to keep a machine is a decision, not a surprise.

Record the off-rent confirmation

Confirmation number, who you spoke to, how you reached them, and when. Written down at the moment you get it, while you are still standing in the yard.

Follow what is awaiting pickup

Confirmed off rent is not the same as collected. Equipment stays on the Awaiting pickup list until you record that it actually left.

Document meter, fuel and condition

Photograph the hour meter, the fuel gauge and the condition at hand-back, with the reading you noted. The state of the machine when you finished with it, kept with the rental.

Review the final invoice

The app compares the invoice against the terms you confirmed and flags a possible mismatch — an extra rental day, a rate that is not the one you entered — for you to look at.

How it works

From delivery to the final invoice.

One workflow, six steps. You stay in control of every one of them.

  1. Add or scan the rental

    Type it in, or scan the rental contract with the camera. Text recognition runs on your iPhone; every value it reads waits for you to confirm it before anything is saved.

  2. Watch the cost and the timing

    Estimated rent running, the next rate change, and how long the machine has been on the ground. Based on the terms you confirmed.

  3. Mark it done, then make the call

    You contact the rental company — the app opens your dialler or an email draft. It does not contact anyone for you.

  4. Record the vendor confirmation

    Confirmation number, representative, method and time. Optionally a one-off location. The rental moves to Vendor confirmation recorded, and the estimate stops accruing there.

  5. Track it until it is collected

    Awaiting pickup, until you record the pickup with the meter reading, the fuel level and photographs of the condition.

  6. Check the invoice, export if you need to

    When the final invoice arrives, attach it and review. If you decide to question a charge, export a PDF packet with the timeline, the confirmation and the photographs.

OffRent Ledger does not contact rental companies and does not end rentals. It can open your dialler or start an email draft, but you make the contact. Nothing recorded in the app is communicated to any rental company, and nothing in it changes your rental agreement.

Privacy and field use

It all stays on your iPhone.

Not a policy position — an architecture. There is no server, so there is nowhere for your rentals, documents, photographs or location to go.

  • No account, no sign-up, no password
  • No server — there is nowhere for your data to go
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDK
  • No advertising and no ad identifier
  • Text recognition runs on your iPhone, never in a cloud
  • Works with no signal, in a yard or a basement
  • Location is one optional reading, only when you tap for it
  • Export a CSV, a JSON backup or a PDF at any time — not behind the subscription
