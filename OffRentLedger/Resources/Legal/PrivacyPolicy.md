# Privacy Policy

**OffRent Ledger** · Idlery Services LLC
Effective date: on first App Store release. Version 1.0.

## The short version

OffRent Ledger keeps everything you enter on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no
analytics. We do not collect your rentals, your documents, your photos or your location, because
there is nowhere for them to go.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Idlery Services LLC receives no data from this app.

The app has no login, no user account, no backend service, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK,
no attribution SDK, no third-party crash reporter and no tracking of any kind. It does not use the
Advertising Identifier, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

If you email our support address, we receive whatever you choose to put in that email. That is a
normal email, handled outside the app.

## What stays on your device

- Rentals, vendors, jobsites, agreements, timeline events, invoices and follow-ups, in a database
  in the app's own storage area.
- Photographs and documents you scan, choose or import, in the app's own file area, protected by
  iOS file protection.
- Reminder settings and your subscription status, in the app's preferences.

Anything the app writes is inside its own container, which iOS keeps separate from other apps.
Deleting OffRent Ledger removes all of it.

## Permissions, and why

**Camera** — requested the first time you tap a scan or photo button. Used to scan rental
contracts and invoices and to photograph equipment condition, meters and fuel gauges. Scanning and
text recognition happen entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No image and no
recognised text is ever sent anywhere.

**Photos** — the app uses the system photo picker, which lets you choose specific images without
granting access to your library. If you ask the app to save a file back to Photos, iOS asks for
add-only permission at that moment.

**Location** — requested only when you tap "Add current location" while recording an off-rent
confirmation or a pickup. It takes one reading, in the foreground, at approximately 100-metre
accuracy, and stores it with that single record. The app does not monitor your location in the
background, does not keep any route or location history, and does not request always-on access.
Declining does not block anything.

**Notifications** — requested only after you turn on a reminder in Settings. Reminders are
scheduled locally on your iPhone. The app receives no push notifications and has no server that
could send one.

## What we do not do

- No data leaves your device for us.
- No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers.
- No analytics or telemetry, first-party or third-party.
- No crash reporting SDK.
- No cloud OCR, machine learning service, or AI service of any kind.
- No selling or sharing of data, because we hold none.
- No profiling and no automated decision-making about you.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. OffRent Ledger asks StoreKit whether you have an active
subscription and stores that answer on your device. We never see your payment details, your Apple
Account, or any personal information about you. Apple's own privacy policy governs the purchase
itself.

## Your data, your control

- Delete any individual attachment.
- Delete any rental, which deletes its events, photos and documents.
- Delete everything, from Settings › Data and privacy, which removes every record and every file
  from this iPhone.
- Export a CSV summary, a full JSON backup, and a per-rental PDF at any time. Exporting is not
  behind a subscription.

Because there is no server, deletion on your device is deletion everywhere. It also means we
cannot recover anything for you. Export before you delete the app.

## Children

OffRent Ledger is a tool for construction businesses. It is not directed at children and collects
nothing from anybody.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships inside the app and its effective date changes.
The app's privacy label on the App Store is kept consistent with this document.
