Support

Email support@idlery.com. OffRent Ledger collects no diagnostics of its own, so please describe what you were doing and what happened.

Does OffRent Ledger contact my rental company?

No. It never has. It helps you record what you did, reminds you to get a confirmation number, and keeps the evidence together. Calling the yard is still your job.

Are the amounts what I owe?

No. They are estimates built from the rates and dates you entered. Your invoice and your rental agreement are what count.

Does a possible mismatch mean I was overcharged?

No. It means an invoice differs from the terms you told the app about. That could be the vendor, your entry, or a term the app cannot see.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Export from Settings before deleting the app — we hold no copy and cannot recover anything for you.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Nothing you have is removed or hidden. You keep editing, resolving, exporting and deleting everything. You just cannot open a new rental beyond the free limit.

OffRent Ledger is a record-keeping tool. It does not contact rental companies and does not end rentals.
