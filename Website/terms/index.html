1. What this app is

OffRent Ledger is a record-keeping tool for equipment rentals. It helps you write down what you rented, what you were told it costs, when you finished with it, what confirmation the rental company gave you, when it was collected, and how the final invoice compares with the terms you entered.

2. What this app is not — read this part

OffRent Ledger does not contact rental companies and does not end rentals.

The app can open your phone dialler, start an email draft, or open a link you saved. You make the contact. Nothing you do in this app is communicated to any rental company, and nothing in it changes your rental agreement.

Specifically, OffRent Ledger does not and cannot:

  • notify a vendor that you are finished with equipment;
  • terminate, suspend or modify a rental;
  • guarantee that a vendor will accept anything you record here;
  • determine that any charge is incorrect, unauthorised or unlawful;
  • provide legal, accounting, insurance, tax or contract advice;
  • produce anything that is legally binding on anybody, or that is legal evidence.

Your rental company's own agreement governs your rental. If the app and your agreement disagree, your agreement is what counts.

3. Estimates

Amounts the app calculates are estimates, produced from rates and dates that you entered. They are not invoices, not statements of account, and not a determination of what you owe. Vendor billing practices vary; the app deliberately does not attempt to interpret contract clauses, choose between rate tiers on your behalf, or calculate overtime or excess-hour charges.

4. Scanning and text recognition

Scanning is a convenience. Text read from a document may be wrong, and the app always asks you to review and confirm every value before it is saved. You are responsible for checking what you save against the underlying document. The document controls, not the scan.

5. Possible mismatches

When the app reports a "possible mismatch", it means an invoice differs from terms you told the app about. That is a prompt to look, and nothing more. It is not an accusation, a finding, or advice about what to do. Deciding whether to accept a charge, question it, or dispute it is entirely yours.

6. Evidence packets

The PDF the app assembles is a summary of information you entered and files you captured, with the dates you recorded. Timestamps reflect when information was entered on your device. They are not independently witnessed and are not legally binding. File checksums are an integrity aid only; they are not a chain of custody and prove nothing about a file's origin. Review anything you generate before sending it to anyone.

7. Your data and your responsibility

Everything you enter is stored on your device. There is no server copy and no backup we hold. Losing, resetting or replacing your device without exporting first means losing your records. You are responsible for your own backups and for the accuracy of what you enter.

OffRent Ledger is free to use for one open rental at a time, with the complete workflow available on it. OffRent Ledger Pro is an auto-renewing subscription offered monthly or annually, sold through Apple.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Ending a subscription never removes or hides anything you have already recorded. You keep viewing, editing, resolving, exporting and deleting all of it. Without Pro you cannot open an additional rental beyond the free limit.
